Freight billing checks

Public repo

Identify billing exceptions before review.

Challenge
Completed shipments can be missing an invoice, incorrectly billed or unpaid.
My responsibility
Shipment-to-invoice reconciliation and a searchable billing ledger.
Approach
Apply the rate valid on the shipment date, select the relevant invoice and combine payments before checking balances.
Decision supported
Which billing exceptions need investigation; which balances need collection follow-up.
Evidence
The demonstration identifies 453 review items across 4,000 generated shipments. The potential billing gap is not recovered revenue.

Challenge

My responsibility

05

Freight capacity planning

Public repo

Plan for the week ahead.

Challenge
Too much reserved capacity wastes money; too little can require expensive extra capacity.
My responsibility
Forecast evaluation and a weekly capacity planning view.
Approach
Compare forecasts with simpler baselines, include possible new sales and vary capacity assumptions.
Decision supported
How much capacity to reserve and how to balance cost against uncovered demand.
Evidence
Six routes evaluated over 12 held-out weeks: 5.72% aggregate forecast error. Interval coverage was about 79%, below the 90% target. All costs and demand are simulated.
